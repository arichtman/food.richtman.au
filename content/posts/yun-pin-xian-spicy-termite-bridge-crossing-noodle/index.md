Rating: 5

Packets!
Double noodles, two kinds of sauce, dehydrated veg, wet veg, mushrooms?!
Spice powder?

One of the sauces smells quite distinctive, might be the termite mushroom.
I found out the mushroom is collybia albuminosa.
I wasn't able to find a picture of the packet I had, but it was very similar to
[the other Yun Pin Xian crossing-the-bridge noodles](../yun-pin-xian-spicy-bridge-crossing-noodle), only red.

It's great!
Spicy, lots of veg and texture, plenty of seasoning.
Noodles are good chew.
My mouth is seriously buzzing.
This is definitely a winner over the other type, which has mustard green instead of the hot oil.
