Rating: 3

Just flakes and soup powder.
Flakes has carrot flecks and mushroom chunks.
Also tiiiny soy pieces.
Noodles don't looks special at all, maybe a bit stiffer than usual?
I had a hard time separating them in the pot.

"Soup" mix is very _red_, like a toppokki sauce - all gochujang/gochugaru.
It's a bit spicy, peppery and warming.
The noodle texture was a little different, more supple?
It's still got wheat flour in it though.
