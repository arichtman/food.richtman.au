Rating: 4

Slightly wider than usual noodles.
Huge dry powder sachet, also a seasoning oil sachet, and egg crumble topping.
Seasoning oil looks like ra-yu.
The egg crumble looks like yolk but crunchy - like pollen.

Something smokey and spiced in here.
Good bit of kick and there is something I can't put my finger on.
