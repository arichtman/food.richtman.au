Rating: 4

Usual load of packets, with pure rice noodles.
Spring onion flake, toasted sesame seeds, tofu skin swirls and dried cabbage,
bean shoots and some very, *very* thick pickled mustard.
Of course a hot oil/bean paste pack.

The hot oil is quite spicy.
It's got whole Sichuan peppercorns and slices of toasted chillis.
Noodles are pretty normal stuff.

It's definitely more real food than your average noodle packet, but not as much as some hot pot ones go.
The variety of ingredients and texture is good, but not great.
The heat I really like but overall it's not that delicious.
If pickled mustard is your thing, this would be a stand-out choice.
